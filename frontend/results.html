Significance & Causation Guidance

Scientific guidelines on interpreting p-values and observational correlations.

Understanding the p-value

A statistically significant trend (p < 0.05) indicates that the observed slope is highly unlikely to have occurred solely through random atmospheric noise or cyclical fluctuations.

Statistical Significance ≠ Causation

Establishing that an environmental variable is increasing or decreasing over a 20-year satellite record does not automatically demonstrate causality. Observational trends may be influenced by multiple interconnected Earth system feedbacks, land use conversions, or unmodeled atmospheric teleconnections.
